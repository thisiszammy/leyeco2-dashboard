# Leyeco II — Transaction Monitor console

The console's design system is the HRIS's. **Read `C:\Users\EXTRASERVER\source\repos\leyeco2-hris\Leyeco2HRIS.Client\CLAUDE.md` before any UI change**: density, the tokens, the components, the overlay portal, per-screen access and the paginated-table conventions all hold here as written there, with `MonitorPolicies` for `HrisPolicies` and `MonitorAccess` for `HrisAccess`. This file records only what differs or is new.

## What was carried over

- `wwwroot/css/app.css`: the HRIS's, whole, less the sections of its own screens (onboarding, the 201 file, the verdict, line-item and conforme palettes). Its "201 edit form grid" is kept as `.edit-form-grid` (§15). A rule both consoles share is changed in both.
- `Components/Shared`: `Avatar`, `Icon` + `IconLibrary`, `KpiCard` (with an `amber` icon tone added here), `NoAccess`, `Pagination`, `PasswordInput`, `Tabs`, `Toasts`, `VersionLabel`. The pickers (`SearchPicker`, `DepartmentPicker`) and `anchored-popover.js` stay in the HRIS until a screen here needs them.
- `Layout/`: the shell as the HRIS draws it (sidebar with rail, no top header, the account at the sidebar's foot, the status line).
- `Features/Authentication`: the sign-in page and its set-a-new-password step, Change password.
- `Features/Users`: the HRIS's Users screen (`Features/SystemConfig` there), at `/administration/users`. The access meter draws one segment per group of `MonitorPolicies.Groups`, two today.

## What is this console's own

- **Pencil for what is planned.** The HRIS draws what is only suggested or not yet on file dashed (pencil) and what is recorded solid (ink). A section under construction uses the same convention at page scale: `Features/OnlinePayments/Components/ReconciliationPlan` frames every figure and register reconciliation will carry in dashed `--slate-300`, named and placed where it will sit, sketched in the quietest slates and `aria-hidden` (words in `.sr-only` say what each will hold), and puts the one true statement, that it is being built, in an ink panel with the gold top rule of a headline panel. Nothing in a sketch is a figure. When the data arrives, a frame turns to ink (a plain `.panel` or `.kpi-strip`) and its sketch is replaced: the plan began as the whole Dashboard, and its volume chart and sources list became Online Payments' ink.
- **The dashboards** (`Features/Dashboard/Pages/Dashboard`, the collections ledger by channel; `Features/OnlinePayments/Pages/OnlinePayments`, the online payment facility). Both inherit `Components/Shared/DashboardPage<T>`: the period lives in the address (`?from=yyyy-MM-dd&to=yyyy-MM-dd`, none for today), a newer pick cancels the read it replaces, a period that includes today is read again every two minutes, and a failed re-read keeps the figures on screen with an amber line saying they are the earlier ones. `PeriodPicker` (`Components/Shared`) is the toolbar control: Today, Yesterday, 7 days, This month, Last month, two dates, the time the figures were read and Read again. Key figures write pesos to the centavo, so their context line goes under the figure (`::deep .stat-body` on each page) and says a change in words ("Up 13.6% on the 21 days before"), never by colour. `Infrastructure/Formatting/Figures` writes every figure, culture-free.
- **Charts** (`Components/Shared/Charts`). `StackedColumns`: columns split by series with 2px white gaps, the first series on the baseline, a y axis of round steps (`ChartMath.Ticks`), one tab stop whose arrow keys walk the columns (a tooltip beside the column and a live region read it), and a Table toggle that swaps the plot for its figures, footed. `SplitBar`: one whole in parts, 24px, each share printed inside where it fits; its key is the register beside it. `Compact` draws it 18px, on a register row's 19px line, so a row of bars stays a 32px row. A chart's colours are the `--series-1…6` tokens (app.css), checked as a set for colour-blind separation in that order: the channels take them in order (Teller, SBA, ECPay, Bayad Center, SM Payment, Bizlink) and keep them on every page; outcomes wear the status tones instead. Gold sits under 3:1 on white, so a chart that draws it always has its table.
- **The Dashboard's By town** (between the chart row and the collectors): Tacloban, Palo, Babatngon and Others, by the code each account number begins with, always all four. Towns take no colour of their own, since the series colours are the channels'. A row is headed ledger style, its code a gray badge in a 28px slot so the names line up (Others and the total leave it empty), then Accounts, Bills, Amount, Share and the change in the amount in words (`Figures.ChangeBrief`, under a head that names what it is against, "vs. 6 days before"), then "Paid through": the town's channel mix as a compact `SplitBar`, so reading down the column compares how the towns pay, and the footing's bar is all towns' mix. "By channel" swaps the summary for the same amounts as a town × channel matrix, footed, a nil written "—", its Total column ruled off: the bars' table. The register scrolls sideways inside its panel when narrower than its figures; at 640px or narrower its Town column stays pinned while the figures scroll under it.
- **A code leads its name** (`.coll-code`, the Dashboard's registers): a 28px slot ahead of the name, so the names line up down the column. A town's code ("01 Tacloban") and a collector's, the two-character `collector_id` the cashiering system stamps on each bill (`Lib_Collectors` there names it: AK is shiela, AC ecpay, BC bayadcntr). A collector whose bills carry none shows "--" in the slot, unboxed in `--slate-500` so it reads as an absence, with "No code" for screen readers; Others and the total leave the towns' slot empty.
- **Ledger footing** (`.ledger-foot`, app.css §20b): a register's total row takes a single rule over the figures and a double rule under the sum, in ink. Only a row that sums the rows above it.
- **The sign-in hero** keeps the HRIS's navy panel and seal, and in place of its feature list draws what the monitor does: two ledgers (Recorded, Settled) ruled like the registers, a gold tick on the margin rule for each matched pair and one entry flagged red with its settlement's place left in pencil. The ticks land once on load (`ledger-mark-in`), the page's one moment of motion; reduced motion shows them at once.
- **The Transaction Inspector** (`Features/TransactionInspector`, `/transaction-inspector`, Inspect transactions) follows each bill paid online through three books: the facility, its posting queue, the collections ledger. It inherits `DashboardPage<T>`; the view, provider, search and page go with each read, and the API cuts them from one cached read of the period, so paging costs the sources nothing.
  - *The passage* (`InspectionPassage`) stands where the dashboards' key figures do and replaces them: one panel with the gold top rule of a headline panel, the three books in posting order (an `<ol>`), each with its headline (what the facility recorded as paid, what it handed to the poster, what the ledger posted under the payments' own receipts) over its statuses. A chevron in a disc sits on each column rule for the hand-off. The ledger closes on how far it stands from the facility, in words. Narrow, the books stack and the chevrons point down.
  - *Tones* (`Models/InspectionTones`): ink for what a book records as it should (Successful, Posted), amber for what waits, red only for a failure, slate for a payment that never went through, and **pencil** (a dashed frame or dot in `--slate-300`/`400`) for a record a book doesn't hold: "Not queued", "Not in the ledger". The verdict is the row's one coloured word: green tallied, amber waiting, red needs attention, slate not paid.
  - *The register* (`InspectionRegister`): a bill a 32px row: the margin, the account and consumer, the bill's period, the payment (reference and time), then each book's entry (status left, amount at the entry's right edge, so amounts line up down each book), the verdict, Open. **The margin is the auditor's**: a gold tick on the gold rule for a bill tallied in all three books (the sign-in hero's tick, for real), a red cross for one needing attention, a pencil ring for one waiting; `aria-hidden`, the verdict says it. **Ditto**: a payment's bills run together behind dotted rules, the payment faded on the rows after its first. Columns are fixed (`table-layout: fixed`, measured widths); the account takes the rest, 220px at least. The row needs 1,252px, the page beside the rail at 1366px. While the register has that, its two rows of heads stick to the page. Narrower, it scrolls sideways in its panel with the margin and the account pinned, or the margin alone at 640px or less (a container query on the panel, so it follows the rail too). A row, or its Open button, opens the bill.
  - *The bill* (`InspectedBillModal`, 1040px): the verdict in a sentence (`InspectionTones.Statement`), then a slip per book side by side, labels in a margin left of the gold rule, totals footed, a book holding nothing left as a pencil slip saying so. "All bills of this payment" searches the payment. The title takes focus on opening; closing returns it to the row's Open.
  - *Exports* sit at the filter bar's far end, since they print what the filters leave: the summary and every bill of the view, as PDF or Excel (the root `CLAUDE.md`, *Printouts*), named as the API names them. `wwwroot/js/files.js` (`monitorFiles.saveAs`) is the HRIS's `appFileIo.saveAs` under this console's name.
- **Negative pesos** write their sign ahead of the currency, "−₱424.28" (`Figures.Peso`): the ledger keeps corrections as negative amounts.
- **Home is per account.** `MonitorAccess.HomePath` (`MonitorPolicies.HomeFor`): the Dashboard, else Online Payments, else the Transaction Inspector, else Users. The sign-in page lands there, the sidebar hides the Dashboard link from an account without its grant, and `NoAccess` offers the way home.
- **Narrow windows open on the rail.** With no sidebar choice remembered (`localStorage["leyeco2.monitor.sidebar"]`), a window 768px or narrower opens the sidebar as the 52px rail (`wwwroot/js/layout.js`, `monitorLayout.isNarrow`), so a phone keeps 320px for the page. A remembered choice wins.
- **The build credit is a signatory line**: "Developed by:" in the label's voice, the two names in ink, each held whole (`white-space: nowrap`) so a short line folds before "& …" and never inside a name, the second name under the first. On the sign-in it is a block: the label row, the version at its far end, over the names. In the status line it is one row while the copyright can give way (the credit does not shrink above 640px). At 480px or narrower it folds onto two rows, and `MainLayout.razor.css` raises `--statusbar-h` to 36px on `.app`, so the sidebar's version cell grows with the footer and the band's top rule still runs straight.
- **The sidebar's groups start open** while it holds a handful of destinations (`NavMenu._expanded` is seeded with every group).
- **A password an administrator sets is handed over in its dialog.** After a save, Reset/Set Password (`UserPasswordModal`, `Issued`) stays open as "Hand Over the Password". The confirmation replaces the toast, and a ruled slip holds the username and the password, each with Copy. The labels sit in a margin left of the gold rule, as on the access sheet, and the password is set a size up, digits in navy and symbols in `--gold-dark`; one click selects it. Closing the dialog drops the password (`UsersList.CloseDialogs`), since nothing else shows it. Copy goes through `wwwroot/js/clipboard.js` (`monitorClipboard.copy`), which falls back to `execCommand('copy')` where `navigator.clipboard` is missing (plain http on the intranet), and the dialog says so if both fail. The HRIS's dialog still closes on save; Add User here still does too.
